--- layout: home title: Andrea Ortega Trujillo subtitle: --- Hello! I am Andrea, a third year PhD Student at the Spanish and Portuguese Programm at UMass Amherst.

I also earned my MA in Advanced Studies in Spanish Language and Linguistics from University of Granada in Spain. Before starting my PhD, I gained experience as a language teacher while working at High Schools in Spain.

I'm mainly interested in how language is influenced by society, and in how our social representation is shaped by and intertwined with linguistic ideologies. My work focuses on sociolinguistics, social behavior, and linguistic practices.
